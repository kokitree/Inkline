A fixed type size is a guess. It can be right for a laptop at arm's length and wrong for a phone on a train, or for anyone whose eyes tire in the evening.

## Three controls are enough

Size, width, and typeface cover most needs. Larger text helps with eyesight. A narrower column helps with focus. A sans-serif face can be easier on some screens and for some readers.

## Make them stick

Settings that reset on every page are a chore. Inkline stores your choices in the browser and applies them before the page paints, so there is no flash of the default style.

## Respect the browser

All sizes are in relative units, so a larger default font in your browser settings still works. Nothing here fights the tools readers already use.
